HOW TO PLAY

🎰 THE BASICS

Pull the lever to spin the reels. Every spin lands you on a challenge tile — a dare, a social task, a drink, or a gamble. Complete it to earn XP and coins. Keep crawling, level up, and grow your brewery.

🪙 HOW YOU MAKE MONEY

Two streams: coins from completed tiles (paid out win or lose) and the brewery, which drips beer coins passively every minute. Level up and buy relics to increase your brew rate — the animated brewery at the top shows your current output.

💎 RELICS & ITEMS

Relics are permanent buffs from the shop — they last the whole crawl and boost brewery rate. Items are one-use consumables — tap USE in the bag when ready. Some are hidden until you trigger them naturally.

🔁 REROLLS

Not happy with a tile? Spend a reroll to skip it and spin again. Your reroll count is in the BAG tab.

🏆 BOSS TILES

High-stakes challenges with big XP. Lose one and it marks you for revenge — beat it later for +50% XP. Winning a boss also opens the shop.
